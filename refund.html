Returns and refunds

Last updated 8 November 2026

We want every school and centre to be happy with Amble Beats. Here is what happens if something is not right.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any time before we dispatch your order and we will refund the full amount. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 85111 49747, or email sales@invengic.in, with your order number.

If it arrives damaged or does not work

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos or a short video. We will repair or replace it at no cost to you, or refund you if a replacement is not possible.

Changing your mind after delivery

You can return an unused Amble Beats in its original packaging within 7 days of delivery. We arrange the pickup; the return shipping cost of ₹X is deducted from your refund.

Faults during the warranty

Manufacturing defects are covered for 1 year from delivery. See the warranty section in our terms of sale.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it. Your bank or card issuer usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.
