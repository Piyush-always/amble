Made for the pre-primary classroom

Counting, colours, patterns and listening become something children do with their whole body. Every step gets an instant sound, so they hear the moment they get it right.

  • Count & PlayCount the steps, then play that many notes.
  • Colour DiscoveryFind the called colour and play it.
  • Pattern BuilderCopy a sound pattern, then add one more.
  • Memory LadderRepeat the tune, one note longer each round.
  • Freeze & PlayMove while the music plays, freeze when it stops.
  • Make Your MelodyChoose notes one after another and play a tune.

How it works

No app, no screen time and nothing to set up between lessons. Switch it on and it is ready to play. It can run on a power bank, so it goes wherever the class is.

1

Choose a mode

Turn the knob to pick Sargam or Drum set. The screen shows which one is on.

2

Move near a sensor

A child waves a hand near one of the eight sensors.

3

Hear it straight away

The built-in speakers play that sensor's note or drum as soon as it picks up the movement.

4

Play again

Each sensor keeps its sound, so children can learn it, predict it and build on it.

What is Amble Beats?
