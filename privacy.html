Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect on this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers amble.invengic.in and is operated by Invengic Ventures Pvt. Ltd.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, organisation, email, mobile number, delivery address, GSTIN if you give one, any notes you add, and the order and payment references.
  • When you send an enquiry: the details you type into the form, such as your name, organisation, phone, email, city and message.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp, call or email: what you send us.

We do not collect card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay collects these directly when you pay.

Why we use it

  • To process and deliver your order, issue invoices and provide warranty support.
  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange demos or quotations.
  • To keep the records that tax and accounting law requires.

We do not sell your data, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, to take your payment.
  • Our delivery partner, to deliver your order: your name, phone and address only.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the order system, and Resend, which sends order notifications to our team.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Videos on this website

Product videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may set cookies when you play a video. See Google's privacy policy for details.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Enquiries are kept for two years from our last contact with you, then deleted.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or erase it, subject to what the law requires us to keep. You can also withdraw consent and raise a grievance. Write to us at sales@invengic.in.
